## Description

Here's an uncomfortable truth: the data team isn't coming to save you.

While requests sit in backlogs and transformation programmes promise future value, people across the business are already solving problems with the tools they have. They always have been. From spreadsheets and databases to modern analytics platforms and low-code tools, the most impactful innovations often start outside formal data teams.

The question isn't whether business users should be building solutions. They're doing it anyway. The real question is whether your organisation is helping them succeed or forcing them into the shadows.

In this session, we'll challenge the belief that every data problem requires a specialist team and explore what happens when organisations trust people closest to the problem to become part of the solution. Through real-world stories, you'll learn why some self-service initiatives create innovation while others create chaos, and how governance can be an enabler rather than a blocker.

You'll leave with practical ideas for reducing bottlenecks, increasing adoption, and building a culture where data ownership doesn't stop with the data team. Because the organisations getting the most value from data aren't the ones with the biggest data teams—they're the ones where everyone feels empowered to solve problems.
