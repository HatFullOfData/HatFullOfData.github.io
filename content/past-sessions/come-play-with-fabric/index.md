## Description

This one‑day, hands‑on workshop is a practical introduction to Microsoft Fabric, designed for anyone who wants to get started with no previous Fabric experience required.

Throughout the day, you’ll explore the core building blocks of Fabric, including Lakehouses, Warehouses, Dataflows Gen2, Notebooks, and Pipelines finishing with lets build an Agent. The focus is on understanding what each component is, why it exists, and how they work together to support modern analytics.

You’ll spend the majority of the workshop learning by doing—ingesting data, transforming it, creating semantic models, and building reports using Fabric and Power BI.

The sessions are designed to be approachable, exploratory, and demo‑rich, giving you the confidence to experiment and continue learning after the workshop. Come along, have a go, and get inspired by what Microsoft Fabric makes possible.
