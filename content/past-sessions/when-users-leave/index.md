**The Ripple Effect Across Microsoft 365 and Power Platform**

## Description

What really happens when a user leaves your organisation? From orphaned Power Automate flows to inaccessible OneDrive files, the departure of a single user can trigger a cascade of issues across Microsoft 365 and the Power Platform. In this practical and eye-opening session, we'll explore the hidden impact of user departures and deleted accounts.

Join us as we:
Trace the lifecycle of a user account from active to deleted
Highlight common pitfalls and data loss risks across Teams, SharePoint, Power BI, and more
Demonstrate how to identify and reassign ownership of critical assets
Share best practices for governance, automation, and user offboarding

Whether you're an admin, consultant, or solution builder, this session will equip you with the knowledge and tools to ensure continuity, compliance, and control when users move on.
